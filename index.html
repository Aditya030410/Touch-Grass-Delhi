WHO CAN PARTICIPATE?
School students under 18 can participate in TOUCH GRASS.
HOW MANY PEOPLE CAN BE ON A TEAM?
Teams can have 1–4 members.
DO WE KNOW THE PROBLEM STATEMENTS BEFOREHAND?
No. The problem statements will be revealed on the day of the hackathon.
WHAT ARE WE SUPPOSED TO BUILD?
Participants will create software-based solutions to the problem statement they receive.
HOW MUCH DOES IT COST TO PARTICIPATE?
Nothing. TOUCH GRASS is completely free to participate in.
DO I NEED TO BRING MY OWN LAPTOP?
Yes. Participants should bring their own laptop, as teams will be building software solutions during the hackathon.
